Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application "VibeCheck drinking game" (package com.vibegame.app). The app is provided as a freemium service with optional rewarded advertising and an optional auto-renewing annual Vibe Pro subscription.

1. Data Collection & Storage

We (the developer) do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our own servers.

Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device using your device's internal storage:

  • Player Names: Names entered during game setup.
  • Custom Content: "Custom Cards" text created by you or generated via AI.
  • App Settings: Volume preferences, game mode configurations, and "Sober Mode" status.
  • API Keys: If you provide a personal Google Gemini API key for AI Deck Forge, it is stored in the app's local data on your device. Do not save a key on a device you do not trust.

This data remains on your device. The app's Delete All Data control or Android's clear-app-data control removes it. Uninstall behaviour may also depend on Android or Google backup settings. We do not operate an account or backup server.

2. Network Access & Third-Party Services

Core local gameplay does not require our own server. Internet access is used for rewarded ads, streamed Party Radio, optional AI Deck Forge, and Google Play purchase verification or restoration. These third-party services may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used for optional rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process device or account identifiers, an IP address (which may estimate approximate location), app interactions, ad events and diagnostic performance information for advertising, measurement, security and service operation. Consent choices determine whether eligible ads are personalised.
  • Google Play Services: Used for the optional annual Vibe Pro subscription, purchase status, restoration and licence verification. Google Play handles payment details; we do not receive card details on our own servers.
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): Used only when a Vibe Pro user initiates AI Deck Forge. The app sends a fixed generation request and the user-provided API key directly to Google. Vibe Check does not proxy or receive the key. Gemini account usage charges may apply.
  • Pixabay CDN: Party Radio streams selected licensed tracks from Pixabay's content-delivery network. The remote service necessarily receives connection information such as IP address when a track is requested.

3. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adults (17+ / 18+ depending on jurisdiction) and contains mature themes. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

4. GDPR & Data Consent (EEA/UK Users)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland, we request your consent to display personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.

Revoking Consent

You have the right to withdraw your consent for personalized advertising and data tracking at any time. To do so, please follow these steps within the application:

  1. Open the VibeCheck drinking game app.
  2. Navigate to the Settings menu (gear icon).
  3. Tap Cookie Settings.
  4. Update your preferences or select "Do Not Consent" in the provided form.

Changing consent may affect ad personalisation. Rewarded ads remain optional and are only requested when you choose an ad-supported unlock.
