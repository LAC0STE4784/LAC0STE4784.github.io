1. Data Collection & Storage

Ordinary gameplay data remains local to your device. We do not operate a VIBE account, cloud gameplay profile, or backup service for player names, saved decks, custom cards, settings or gameplay state. First-party collection occurs when you explicitly submit a card report or contact the developer for support; ordinary gameplay is not uploaded to a VIBE gameplay server.

Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device using your device's internal storage:

  • Player Names: Names entered during game setup.
  • Custom Content: "Custom Cards" text entered by you, including text you may choose to create with a separate AI service.
  • Purchase and advertising state: Cached Google Play entitlement/receipt metadata and advertising-consent choices.
  • App Settings: Volume preferences, game mode configurations, and "Sober Mode" status.
  • Legacy AI Keys: Current builds do not ask for, read or use Gemini API keys. If an older Vibe build saved one, Settings can remove that unused legacy key.
  • Card-report drafts: Reports you save before submission, plus local delivery status and any returned server receipt.

Clearing app data removes local information; uninstall behaviour may also depend on Android or Google backup settings. We do not operate our own general-purpose gameplay backup server.

Card Reports Submitted to VIBE

Saving a report draft does not upload it. If you explicitly press Submit report, the app sends the following allowlisted report fields to the developer's first-party reporting endpoint: report ID/time, source classification, selected reason, optional comment, reported card ID/raw text/type/mode/intensity, and app version/build.

Submitted card-report records are stored privately for developer moderation and are scheduled for deletion after 90 days.

The report schema has no separate field for your saved player list or player names and does not automatically include saved decks, any legacy Gemini API key, advertising identifiers, billing/order/account data, precise location, contacts, or device serial. However, the reported card text and optional comment can contain information that you entered, including names or other personal information. Do not include personal or sensitive information unless it is necessary to explain the report. Obvious Google API-key-shaped strings in report card text/comments are redacted before transport and again by the receiver.

A successful in-app submission returns a receipt to the device. Failed/offline reports remain locally retrievable for retry. Reporting a card does not automatically remove it.

You can also choose an email fallback. Opening the email app does not send anything until you press Send there; your message and sender address are then received by the developer through the email provider. Support correspondence is separate from the automated 90-day card-report store. Contact the developer at the address below to request deletion of correspondence; applicable legal obligations may limit deletion.

2. Network Access & Third-Party Services

Core local gameplay and AI Deck Forge prompt export can run without our own gameplay server. An internet connection is required for rewarded ads, streamed Party Radio, Google Play purchase verification/restoration, and in-app card-report submission. We use the following services, which may process data under their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. To provide this service, Google and its partners may collect and process certain data from your device.

  • Data Collected:
    • Identifiers and approximate location: Device or account identifiers and an IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
    • Usage Data: App interaction data, ad views, clicks, and impressions.
    • Diagnostics: App and SDK performance information such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
  • Purpose: This data is used to serve personalized ads (based on your interests and past activity) or non-personalized ads (based on context), to measure ad performance, and to prevent fraud.
  • Opt-Out: You can manage your advertising preferences through your device settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).
  • Google's Advertising Privacy Policy

Google Play Services

Used for optional Vibe Pro purchases—auto-renewing annual Vibe Pro and the non-renewing three-day Party Weekend prepaid plan—plus license verification, restoration, subscription status, and core system functionality.

  • The app reads purchase/receipt metadata from Google Play and uses Play purchase acknowledgement, entitlement and restoration services. Google Play processes transaction information to manage purchases and subscriptions. VIBE does not operate a server-side receipt-validation service and does not receive your payment-card details.
  • Subscription restoration depends on the Google account used for the original purchase. The app caches current entitlement locally but Google Play remains the purchase source.

Optional External AI Use

In build23 (20.3) and the current prompt-export workflow, AI Deck Forge copies one fixed VIBE card-writing prompt to your clipboard. The following describes that workflow:

  • Vibe does not ask for, read or transmit an AI API key, does not contact an AI provider, and does not receive a provider response.
  • If you paste the prompt into an AI service, that is a separate interaction you choose under that provider's terms and any applicable charges.
  • Cards you manually add remain stored locally like other custom cards.

Older versions: Some older Vibe builds and the older web preview offer direct Gemini generation using an API key supplied by you. When you request a deck through that older feature, its generation request and key are sent directly to Google under Google's terms and applicable API charges. VIBE has no AI proxy server. Current builds use the external-prompt workflow; Settings can remove an unused legacy key.

Vercel (First-Party Card Reports)

Vercel hosts VIBE's first-party card-report endpoint and private report storage when you explicitly submit a report. Vercel may process connection/operational data such as IP address and request metadata to deliver and secure the service.

Report Notifications

The developer may use a notification service to alert them to a new accepted report. Notification payloads are limited to report receipt/time/source/reason/app metadata and deliberately exclude the reported card text and optional comment.

Pixabay CDN (Party Radio)

Party Radio streams selected licensed tracks from Pixabay's content-delivery network. The remote service necessarily receives connection information such as your IP address when a track is requested. Party Radio is optional and labelled beta because remote tracks can become unavailable.
